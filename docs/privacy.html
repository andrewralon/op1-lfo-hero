op1 lfo hero

privacy policy  ·  effective june 16, 2026  ·  updated october 19, 2026

no data collected

op1 lfo hero does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. no account is required. no analytics or tracking of any kind is present in the app.

bluetooth

bluetooth is used solely to discover and connect to a teenage engineering op-1 field, tx-6 or tp-7. no data is sent to any server. all communication stays between your phone or tablet and the connected device.

midi

midi messages generated by the app go directly to the connected device over usb or bluetooth. nothing is logged, stored, or sent anywhere else.

settings on your device

the app saves your settings, your lfo chips, and the last bluetooth device you connected to on your own iphone or ipad (in the keychain and the app's local storage). they never leave the device, and deleting the app removes them.

third-party services

the app contains no third-party sdks, advertising networks, or analytics libraries.
