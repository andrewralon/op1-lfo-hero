op1 lfo hero

make music fun - custom midi lfos for the op-1 field by teenage engineering

get it on the app store  ·  iphone, ipad and apple silicon macs

connection

  • connect iphone to op1 via usb-c or bluetooth (ble midi)
  • open the app — automatically connects to op1
  • status bar shows op-1 field (usb) or op-1 field (ble)

tempo modes

  • op1 (beat match) — op1 is master. app's lfos follow op1's tempo.
  • app (midi sync) — app is master. app controls basic op1 tape transport (play/stop/back/forward).
    • tempo lfo requires usb-c connection.
    • note: bluetooth (ble midi) does not send high-resolution tempo changes.

toggle the app's tempo mode by tapping the metronome, then change the op1 to match.

help wanted — got another te device?

every device lfo hero supports was mapped by measuring real hardware, because the published midi references turned out to be wrong in several places. adding the op-1 (original), op-z, ep-133, ob-4 or a po means someone who owns one has to record what it actually sends.

it takes about ten minutes and you don't need to know anything about midi:

  • guided mapper — runs in the browser, walks you through it, produces a file to send. needs chrome, edge or firefox on a computer
  • manual instructions — for safari, or if you'd rather use a midi monitor you already trust
