troubleshooting

  • app shows no device found
    make sure the device is powered on. for usb: try a different cable — some usb-c cables are charge-only and carry no data. for bluetooth: confirm bluetooth: on is set on the op-1, or that the tx-6 / tp-7 is advertising. if it still isn't found, open the device picker and tap it, or pick the device yourself in settings.
  • controls send but the device doesn't respond
    unplug and replug the device, or restart lfo hero. if using bluetooth, toggle bluetooth: off then back on on the op-1.
  • tx-6 ignores the app
    set midi control to in on the tx-6. in that mode it stops sending its own fader and knob messages, which is expected.
  • tx-6 tempo, play and stop do nothing, but volume and mute work
    set clock SRC to usb over usb-c or ble over bluetooth. the tx-6 only accepts clock and play/stop from the source you pick.
  • lfos stopped and the app says lfos paused — no clock
    the app is following the device's clock and none is arriving. tap the banner to use the app's clock instead. a tp-7 only sends clock while its tape is moving, and only in sync mode.
  • tp-7 doesn't respond to the app
    leave ctrl mode — in it the tp-7 acts as a controller for other gear and ignores the app. off and sync both accept volume, mute and gain.
  • a tx-6 fader fights the lfo
    grabbing a fader while an lfo drives that channel makes them compete. pause the lfo chip first.
  • op-1 tape doesn't respond to play / stop
    confirm the op1 is in midi sync mode and the app is in app clock mode. the op1's bpm display should show -- while waiting for clock.
  • device tempo doesn't match the app
    restart the app. power cycle the device. note: bluetooth (ble midi) does not send high-resolution tempo changes.
  • left / right tape buttons stop playback (op-1)
    this is normal op1 behavior — receiving a song position pointer while playing causes it to pause. press play to resume.
